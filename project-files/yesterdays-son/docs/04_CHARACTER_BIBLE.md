## Consistency checklist

- Goal and emotional state are clear in every scene.
- The character knows only what prior scenes allow.
- Costume, age, injuries, props, and location state match chronology.
- Dialogue is distinguishable without speaker labels.
- Visual generations use approved references and record model/tool settings.

## Register

Maintain the sortable master list in production/spreadsheets/Character_Register.xlsx. Character folders should contain README, references, turnarounds, expressions, costumes, poses, prompts, and approvals.
