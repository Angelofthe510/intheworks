## Editorial rules

- Preserve every source generation intact.
- Keep original Suno titles and permanent links for provenance.
- A song may serve both a standalone video and the opera.
- The air-guitar performance remains separate from the multiverse mashup, though it may become an optional meta-epilogue.
- The 1974 Rock cover should evoke an old Italian ruin in monochrome: a weathered stone arch, deep shadow, archaeological age, isolation, and a slightly surreal atmosphere. Do not reuse the current colorful band artwork as the final cover direction.
- Candidate status never implies final approval or replacement of another version.
