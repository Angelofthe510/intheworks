## Original Suno style

High-energy retro-future dance with tight double-kick drum grooves, crisp finger snaps, and aggressively punchy synth bass. Layered MIDI synth leads cut through the mix with bright arpeggios and stuttered hooks, while the low end slams with sidechained sub and distorted mid-bass grit. Drums hit hard with gated snares, sharp claps, and syncopated hi-hats that drive a relentless club-ready rhythm. Vocals stay three-voiced and characterful, sitting upfront with slapback delays and wide chorus effects for an intense, warp-speed party feel.

## Short style label

Retro-future hyperdrive dance

## Reuse guidance

Use this style when a track needs propulsion, theatrical ensemble vocals, and an energetic science-fiction dance identity. Preserve the source song's melody and structure when using it for a Cover. Adjust the vocal instructions separately for the character performing the song.

## Negative guidance

No trap beat, no contemporary festival EDM drop, no slow ambient opening, no muddy bass, no washed-out vocals, no generic cinematic trailer impacts, no long fade-out, and no loss of the original melodic hook.
