# Delivery Checklist

- Correct approved version and filename.
- Picture, sound, color, titles, and credits pass QC.
- Captions/subtitles and accessibility assets included when required.
- Format, codec, resolution, frame rate, sample rate, loudness, and aspect ratio match destination.
- Rights, attribution, likeness/voice, music, and generated-media provenance reviewed.
- Metadata, thumbnail/key art, description, links, and campaign copy approved.
- Source, master, checksum, delivery location, and release date recorded.
- Public output tested after publication.
- Superseded files moved out of active delivery folders.
