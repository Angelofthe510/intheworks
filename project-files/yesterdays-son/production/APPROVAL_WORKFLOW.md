# Approval Workflow

Idea → Draft → In Review → Approved → In Production → Released

Items may also become Blocked, Superseded, or Archived.

## Review dimensions

Creative: serves story and manifesto.
Continuity: matches canon, timeline, and dependencies.
Technical: meets file and delivery specification.
Rights: provenance, permissions, credits, and restrictions recorded.
Release: correct version, metadata, captions/accessibility, destination, and owner.

Record the reviewer, date, notes, and decision in the relevant register. Revisions after approval return the new version to In Review.
