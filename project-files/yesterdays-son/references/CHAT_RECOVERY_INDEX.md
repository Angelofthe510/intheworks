## Recovered creative decisions

### Project shape

The material has three connected layers:

1. **Yesterday’s Son — A Lonely Opera** — the main dramatic narrative.
2. **Character Songs** — including Kirk, Nyota, Borg Spock, and the Enterprise.
3. **World Music Videos** — including Andorian, Deltanite, and Orion pieces.

A song may be both a standalone work and part of the opera. Preserve one source asset and record both intended uses.

### Canonical opera architecture

- Pre-opera cold open: ship ambience, communications interference, fossilized-delta discovery, and Spock’s “Unreal.”
- The 7:33 opera begins at **“Evidence Answers Back,”** after the cold open.
- The middle develops the robotic/Borg signal.
- The ending becomes a glitch coda that loops sonically and visually back to the opening.
- Revised animatic duration: approximately 8:15 — 0:35 cold open, 7:33 opera, 0:07 coda.
- Established visual progression: Prime → Mirror → Borg → decision → passage through the Guardian.
- **Timeline Shift** is the recovered Act I overture/narrative hinge: Enterprise and its computer remember Spock while Kirk, McCoy, and Chapel do not; an unidentified Andorian occupies his station; Spock must return through the Guardian toward Vulcan and the threatened boy who would become him.

### Musical world

- Keep *Captain’s Purr-suasion* as the earlier Orion interpretation.
- Keep *The Heart of a Commander — Disco 1* as a distinct later disco interpretation, not a replacement.
- Preserve the four Lonely Guy/Borg/disco/dance sources as a parts library rather than eliminating all but one.
- The Andorian instrumental can be one cultural interpretation of a shared multiverse musical signal.
- The opening has three developed treatment lanes: 1970s dramatic television mystery; wordless alien “Song of the Son”; recovered transmission/sound design.
- Treatment 2 separates theremin, low xylophone, and wordless Sirens into controllable layers.
- **“Quiet Orbit”** is the recovered ViVa/Nee-La musical-conversation concept.
- Character-language rule: ViVa communicates through piano; Nee-La communicates through saxophone; Spock also has the intimate voice of the Vulcan lyre.

- ViVa's opening point of view is intellectual and artistic fascination, explicitly distinguished from ordinary sexual attraction.
