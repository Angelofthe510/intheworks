# Amanda Grayson — Recovered Character Direction

Status: Recovered approved pitch direction
Source conversation: **Amanda Grayson A-Pose**

Amanda Grayson is Spock's accomplished, intellectually formidable human mother. She built a life on Vulcan without surrendering her humanity. Composed, perceptive, and quietly courageous, she understands Spock's divided identity more deeply than anyone. Where Sarek embodies Vulcan discipline and expectation, Amanda offers emotional clarity. She does not ask Spock to reject logic; she reminds him that compassion is not weakness.

Her design uses elegant simplicity. Amanda wears no jewelry or status symbols. Her dignity comes from intelligence, integrity, and self-possession rather than display. Flowing amber, orange, olive, and green garments introduce warmth into the restrained Vulcan environment.

Within the story, Amanda is Spock's emotional compass and living connection to Earth: the person who recognizes the loneliness beneath his control and the humanity he cannot silence completely.
