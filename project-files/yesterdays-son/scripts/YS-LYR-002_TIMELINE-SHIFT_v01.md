# Timeline Shift

Status: Recovered rough first-pass lyrics
Source: Bluecoats Band conversation, recovered through the Create Yesterday’s Son project handoff
Narrative role: Act I overture / narrative hinge
Arrangement note: originally explored as Broadway, then moved toward contemporary 1970s new wave, anxious synth/art-rock, and theatrical spoken-sung delivery

## Spoken-sung intro

Enterprise. Stardate 5373.4
Everything is exactly where it should be.
Except.

## Verse 1

The ship remembers me
The computer knows my name
But when I look at their faces
They see a stranger in my frame

## Pre-chorus

Kirk asks me “Who are you?”
And I don't know how to answer that
When the past rewrites itself
Where does that leave me at?

## Chorus

Something broke in the timestream
Something died before it lived
Now I'm standing in the shadow
Of a life I never lived
The Guardian showed us history
But history changed its mind
Now I have to go back, back, back
And save myself in time

## Verse 2

McCoy doesn't know my name
Chapel looks right through my face
There's an Andorian at my station
Standing in my place
Cause and effect
Unraveling
Which thread do I pull
To make myself real again?

## Chorus — reprise

Timeline shift, reality drift
I'm a ghost in my own life
Have to go back through the Guardian
Back to Vulcan, back to the knife
Back to the moment where it all went wrong
Before the boy who would be me
Is gone

## Recovered style direction

1970s new wave with anxious energy, synth arpeggios, art rock, and cinematic orchestral accents. Male baritone spoken-sung vocals with theatrical, intellectual delivery; expressive diction; rhythmic speech; animated phrasing; a confident lecture-meets-performance tone; dynamic tempo shifts and controlled tension and release.

## Continuity caution

The Andorian occupying Spock's science station is **not automatically Nee-La**. Earlier production discussion deliberately protected this distinction.
