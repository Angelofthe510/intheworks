# Yesterday's Son — Story Foundation

Status: continuity-audited working foundation.

Sources reviewed: the supplied script *Unreal* and the older `yson.html` pitch overview. The script controls where they conflict.

## Story hierarchy

1. The current *Unreal* script controls plot continuity.
2. The older Yesterday's Son pitch remains emotional and development context.
3. Borg and wider multiverse ideas remain later expansions until explicitly approved into the Opera sequence.

## Stable story spine

- Ancient ruins bear an impossible Enterprise/Starfleet insignia.
- Prime and Mirror Spock pursue parallel investigations.
- The mystery leads toward Zar, Spock and Zarabeth's son from Sarpeidon's past.
- Zar has timeline-locked temporal biology.
- The Guardian of Forever has a larger purpose in connecting events and timelines.
- Borg is the widening threat, not the opening premise.
- The emotional center is fatherhood, love, loss, duty, and sacrifice.
