ORIGINAL SUNO STYLE

Full Production Language

High-energy retro-future dance with tight double-kick drum grooves, crisp finger snaps, and aggressively punchy synth bass. Layered MIDI synth leads cut through the mix with bright arpeggios and stuttered hooks, while the low end slams with sidechained sub and distorted mid-bass grit. Drums hit hard with gated snares, sharp claps, and syncopated hi-hats that drive a relentless club-ready rhythm. Vocals stay three-voiced and characterful, sitting upfront with slapback delays and wide chorus effects for an intense, warp-speed party feel.

REUSE GUIDANCE

When to Deploy

Use when a track needs propulsion, theatrical ensemble vocals, and an energetic science-fiction dance identity. Preserve the source melody and structure when making a Cover. Adjust vocal instructions separately for the character performing the song.

NEGATIVE GUIDANCE

Exclude From Generation

No trap beat, contemporary festival EDM drop, slow ambient opening, muddy bass, washed-out vocals, generic cinematic trailer impacts, long fade-out, or loss of the original melodic hook.
