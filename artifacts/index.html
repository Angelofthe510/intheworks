    "Yesterday's Son" update

    MMAN 20A · From Wednesday's Animation Club/lab · Sunday, November 24, 2024

    • Lab
    • Yesterday's Son
    • AI video
    • After Effects
    • This week's lesson: AI can take more time to use than good ol' After Effects.
    • Tried Hailuo AI image-to-video for a cartoon shot, "ship approaches slo-mo rotating planet," using two images for two angles.
    • The easy way: old-school cel animation. Screenshots from the Animated Series, ship PNGs in Photoshop, space and planet PNGs plus a gradient-strip planet surface in Illustrator, then every piece stacked and animated as rectangles in After Effects.
    • Bonus: a surprise "recreate" prompt turned an existing image of a bearded Vulcan into a short clip.
    • Seven playable clips: four AI tries, two After Effects renders, and the Vulcan.
