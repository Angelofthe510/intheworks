Working motion language

Slow movement. Musical connection.

Current editorial direction is to animate these stills gently, using restrained character motion, slow pushes, and soft dissolves from shot to shot. ViVa’s piano and Neeh-La’s saxophone lead the visual conversation; the quartet hero establishes the shared performance space. The drum concept remains intentionally open while the quartet layout stays production-ready.
